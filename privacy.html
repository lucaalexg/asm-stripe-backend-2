Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Archive Sur Mer Marketplace collects and processes personal data for buyer accounts, seller operations, and payment-related workflows.

1. Data we collect

We may collect account details (name, email, phone), listing information, order references, and support communication records required to operate the marketplace.

2. Why we process data

Data is processed to provide core marketplace functions: authentication, listing moderation, checkout, order management, fraud prevention, and customer support.

3. Data sharing

Payment and payout data may be processed by regulated payment providers. We only share data required to complete transactions and maintain platform compliance.

4. Retention and deletion

We retain data only as long as required for legal, accounting, and operational purposes. You may request access, correction, or deletion by contacting support.

5. Contact for privacy requests

Please contact privacy@archivesurmer-marketplace.com for privacy-related requests.
